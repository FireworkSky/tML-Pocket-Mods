Phone* control sprites and ../MobileMenu/Editor* sprites are extracted from
the connected phone's data.unity3d UI6 atlas using its original UI metadata.
Regenerate with: python tools/prepare_control_editor_assets.py

Source references are kept under obj/mobile-live-reference and obj/unity-atlases.
The output preserves original sprite colors, outlines and transparent corners.
RGB is premultiplied by alpha because tModLoader's PNG-to-rawimg conversion
and RawImgReader preserve RGB while SpriteBatch uses AlphaBlend.

Mount and loadout glyphs are cells from the original 0x5B4C13DC equipment
sheet (34 x 34 pixels per cell); they are not cuts from a phone screenshot.
Loadout colors are applied at draw time. Item buttons use Terraria item assets
and the current held/healing item where applicable.

PhoneEmptyGrappleGlyph is the original 34 x 34 grapple equipment placeholder
cell (column 1, row 1) of 0x5B4C13DC. Equipped grapple artwork uses Terraria's
native QuickGrapple_GetItemToUse item at runtime; the editor action picker
continues to use its separate PhoneGrappleGlyph.
